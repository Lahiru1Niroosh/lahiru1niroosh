## 👨‍💻 About Me

<img align="right" width="280" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif" alt="coding gif"/>

I'm a final-year **BSc IT (Hons)** student at **SLIIT**, currently working as a **Database Administrator at VS Information Systems**, Colombo — where I maintain high-availability production Oracle banking databases, automate monitoring workflows, and perform SQL-based analysis to identify performance bottlenecks.

My passion is bridging the gap between operational DBA work and modern data engineering: designing streaming pipelines with **Kafka & Spark**, building **Medallion architectures**, and turning raw data into real business insights. I'm actively growing toward a **Senior Data Engineer** role.
